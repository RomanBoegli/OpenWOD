## `just-do-it.webm`, `just-do-it.mov`

A cut of one speech from *#INTRODUCTIONS* (2015) by LaBeouf, Rönkkö & Turner, made with Central Saint Martins Fine Art students.
The artists released the work under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike licence ([the work on Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/125095515)).
The two files here are an adaptation: one speech cut out, the green screen replaced by transparency, cropped and scaled to 650 by 464, and encoded twice.
They are shared under the same licence, so they may be passed on and changed with this credit, and not used commercially.
