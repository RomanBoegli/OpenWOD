Released 26 October 2026.
Image: `ghcr.io/romanboegli/openwod:v1.1.0`, digest `sha256:942c398849b29be7f36f759b1f6d930dfa077dbf51d61ee313b33b9c762a9831` (linux/amd64 and linux/arm64).
Pin it in the stack with `APP_IMAGE=ghcr.io/romanboegli/openwod@sha256:942c398849b29be7f36f759b1f6d930dfa077dbf51d61ee313b33b9c762a9831`.

## What is in it

- Everything from 1.0.0: six formats on one deterministic clock, solo or in pairs, recorded voices and beeps, the household server with SQLite, device cache and outbox, recovery after a reload or a killed browser, single-tab control, history with corrections, workout files and packs with provenance, backup and restore, and the editor for every format.
- An accent colour per theme in Settings → General → Appearance: six swatches and a custom picker for the dark theme and the same for the light theme, each shown in the shade that theme will use for buttons, links, the wordmark and the work-phase ring.
- A rest colour per theme under Settings → Training view → Look & feel: six swatches and a custom picker for each theme, driving the ring, the label and the pulse of rest and transition phases and the lane that waits for its partner.
- The prep countdown counts down in For Time and strength workouts too, where the clock had shown 00:00 until the work began, and it wears the rest colour with a draining ring like any transition.
- "Get ready" and the prep countdown are heard on the recorded voice: Start used to reach the audio layer before the browser had resumed the audio output, so the first announcement fell back to the louder device voice and the prep countdown and its Go were never scheduled.
- Next section and Previous section buttons in the player (Shift + arrow keys on a keyboard), in every format. A skipped section is scored as skipped, a revisited one is scored again.
- Skip buttons either side of the transport, as on a video player: they move the clock back or forward by a chosen number of seconds (10 by default, under Training view → Look & feel), the J and L keys do the same, and a forward skip at the end of a slot fires the boundary as if the time had passed.
- Overall progress has a fourth option, Bar + info + %, which also puts the session percentage beside the big clock, smaller and dimmed.
- The overall progress bar is as thick as the timer ring, so the two follow the ring width setting together.
- The athlete columns of the game plan and the context view are headed by the athletes' names alone, without the lane letter.
- The context view's game plan shows the load levels and the notes of every movement, as the focus view does.
- The recorded voices are at one level: every clip is measured and normalised, where the earlier render had left them about 15 dB too quiet with single words up to 14 dB louder than their neighbours. The countdown, Go, the athlete letters and the movement names now sound alike.
- Voice + beeps now means both: the beep marks every countdown second and boundary on the audio clock, and the recorded number or word follows it. Before, a recorded voice replaced the beeps.
- The beeps have their own volume, separate from the voice and much louder at the top of its slider, and one of five tones (Classic, Soft, Sharp, Gym timer with the held 1 kHz beep of a gym clock, and the harsh Alarm) under Settings → Training view → Sound and voice, with a Test beeps button.
- The library is a three-column feed: cards keep their natural height and pack like a pinboard, the collection sits beside the title, and the whole card opens the workout.
- The "Coach programming" collection is now called "Advanced".
- The footer links to the repository and to the release of the running version.
- The sound test prepares the recorded movement names the same way a session does, says so when it had to fall back to the device voice, and a device-voice fallback always speaks English.
- The pre-1.0 storage writers, the unused helpers and the frozen version 1 schema were removed.

## Supported browsers

Tested by the automated suite in WebKit, Firefox and Chromium engines.
Safari on macOS and iOS, Firefox and Chrome are the intended browsers.
Web Locks, BroadcastChannel and IndexedDB are required for recovery and single-tab control.
Browsers without them still play workouts, show "Not saving progress on this device", and never claim a recovery.

## Known limitations

- No login.
  Keep the server on a trusted network or behind an authenticating proxy.
- Session events are uploaded once, with the finished session, not streamed while playing.
- The JSON export can be read but not imported back.
  Restoring is done with the SQLite backup.
- The physical device checks are recorded per release by hand.
- The accent and rest colours, beep level and tone are device preferences, not shared through the household server.

## Upgrading

This is the first release with a database, so there is no upgrade path to run.
A device that still holds a pre-1.0 library offers to move it to the server once, from the library page or from Settings → General.
A browser that saved an accent or other preferences keeps them.
