# OpenWOD 1.5.2

Image `ghcr.io/romanboegli/openwod:v1.5.2` (linux/amd64, linux/arm64), static site at [romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD](https://romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD/) and `openwod-static-v1.5.2.zip` attached.

## What's new

- Workout titles and movement names in Title Case.
- Tidier movement catalogue: redundant aliases removed, short names resolve, Dumbbell Overhead Sit-Ups added, Triceps Dips need no equipment.
- Dumbbell Full Body 35 reworked as Dumbbell Full Body Crunch.
- Firefox keeps the favicon on bookmarks of the household page.

## Upgrading

Set `APP_IMAGE` to `v1.5.2`, re-pull and redeploy. No migration, and rolling back to 1.5.1 is safe.
Movements you edited under Settings → Movements keep your version: Restore the bundled movements there to take the new ones.
