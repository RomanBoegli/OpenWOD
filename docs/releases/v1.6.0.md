# OpenWOD 1.6.0

Image `ghcr.io/romanboegli/openwod:v1.6.0` (linux/amd64, linux/arm64), static site at [romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD](https://romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD/) and `openwod-static-v1.6.0.zip` attached.

## New Features

- Continue a running session on another device: the household server keeps it, and every other device offers Continue here.
- Start begins the session at once, from a card, a workout's page or the logbook.
- The clock and the round donut take all the room a workout leaves, on every screen size.
- Station cards and the game plan are neutral glass, and the Pair and Solo chips take your accent.
- Overall progress is four checkboxes: overall bar, percentage under it, percentage beside the clock, round donut.
- One compact colour control everywhere, a colour for the paused state, and every phase colour per theme.
- Toggles replace the dropdowns for Default view and Sound, and the sound has three tests: voice, beeps, both.
- A new beep tone: the ship horn.
- A workout's page and the editor's summary show Total, Active and Rest time.
- The library sorts by Recently added.
- New in Default: Partner Band & Weight Upper Body Focus, with five more movements and Resistance Band as an equipment name.
- Equipment names are in Title Case, such as Pull-Up Bar, including the ones you stored.
- A movement typed in lower case in the editor takes the catalogue's spelling when you leave the field.
- The guide for AI assistants asks for loads on every weighted movement and keeps notes to a few words.
- Removed: the Clock digits, Clock size and Device voice speed options, the lane caption under an athlete's name, and the example hints in form fields.

## Bugfixes

- The station cards no longer stay green or purple whatever accent is chosen ([#2](https://github.com/RomanBoegli/OpenWOD/issues/2)).
- Editor: typing an amount no longer resets the target or the load, and a comma works as the decimal mark.
- Tapping a suggestion picks it on iPhone and iPad.
- Women's loads survive an edit of the men's loads.
- Switching a draft to Solo no longer erases athlete B's movements.
- An edit made just before leaving the editor is kept, and a save that fails says so.
- The training view fits an iPad in portrait and landscape and follows a turn mid-session.
- A tab that handed its session over no longer reacts to the keyboard.
- Leaving a running session shows it as interrupted at once, without a reload.
- Phones no longer zoom the page when a field takes the focus.

## Upgrading

Set `APP_IMAGE` to `v1.6.0`, re-pull and redeploy.
The database migrates to schema 4 on start, after a backup under `/data/backups`.
Rolling back to 1.5.x needs that backup, since older images refuse a migrated database.
