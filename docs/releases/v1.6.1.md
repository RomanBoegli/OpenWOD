# OpenWOD 1.6.1

Image `ghcr.io/romanboegli/openwod:v1.6.1` (linux/amd64, linux/arm64), static site at [romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD](https://romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD/) and `openwod-static-v1.6.1.zip` attached.

## Bugfixes

- Get ready and the first movement names come in the chosen voice again after Start, not in the device voice.
- The first countdown after Start speaks its numbers and plays the chosen horn.
- Start waits until the sound has loaded, for two seconds at most, and its icon turns meanwhile.

## Upgrading

Set `APP_IMAGE` to `v1.6.1`, re-pull and redeploy. No migration, and rolling back to 1.6.0 is safe.
