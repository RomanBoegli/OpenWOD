# OpenWOD 1.7.0

Image `ghcr.io/romanboegli/openwod:v1.7.0` (linux/amd64, linux/arm64), static site at [romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD](https://romanboegli.ch/OpenWOD/) and `openwod-static-v1.7.0.zip` attached.

## New Features

- Quick timer: a Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP or For Time clock from a few numbers, without building a workout ([#11](https://github.com/RomanBoegli/OpenWOD/issues/11)).
- A quick AMRAP lists every counted round with its time, the latest on top.
- Solo sessions have a layout of their own: one station across the full width, without the athlete badge and the empty second column ([#6](https://github.com/RomanBoegli/OpenWOD/issues/6)).
- Party horn button in the training view, also on the H key ([#14](https://github.com/RomanBoegli/OpenWOD/issues/14)).
- "Just do it" button in the training view, also on the D key ([#15](https://github.com/RomanBoegli/OpenWOD/issues/15)).
- Both buttons are off until you switch on Fun buttons in Settings → Training view.
- The image tag `latest` follows every release from this one on.

## Upgrading

Set `APP_IMAGE` to `v1.7.0`, or to `latest`, then re-pull and redeploy.
No migration, and rolling back to 1.6.1 is safe.
