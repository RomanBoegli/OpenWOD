1) CV Warm-up / Primer – 15 min
Goal: Raise the heart rate, activate shoulders and glutes, wake up the core, and get moving/sweating.
3 rounds for quality – 10 min cap
3/3/3 Muscle Clean + Power Clean + Squat Clean
6/6 Rotational KB Deadlifts
5 Barbell Strict Press
5 Barbell Push Press
20/20/20 Ski or Assault Bike
2) Clean & Jerk – Building Confidence with Fatigued Legs – 20 min
Every 1:30 x 8 sets:
1 Power Clean
1 Front Squat
1 Split Jerk
Coaching focus:
Stay composed even with fatigued legs.
Focus on full extension in the clean.
Keep the front squat upright and controlled.
Reset before the jerk, then drive strong through the legs.
Fast footwork and a stable catch in the split jerk.
Please don’t lift all at the same time.
3) Gymnastics & Core Work – 8 x 40 sec on / 40 sec off
Rotate through:
40 Double-Unders
Toes-to-Bar into 20 sec Core Hold
Rest
Coaching notes:
Focus on a relaxed kip and a proper arch position.
Squeeze the core and work actively through the shoulders.
Keep the midline tight during the core hold.
Quality over quantity.
4) Strength – 4 rounds EMOM
EMOM x 12 min
10 x Double KB Hang Cleans
8 x Chin-Ups
10 x Bench Press
Goal: Build pulling, pressing, and grip strength while keeping the intensity controlled.
Coaching focus:
KB Hang Cleans: strong hip drive, clean catch, no crashing on the forearms.
Chin-Ups: full range of motion and controlled reps.
Bench Press: stable shoulder position, controlled descent, strong lockout.
5) Metcon
From TheBox Programming
EMOM x 5 rounds
21 Wall Balls
15/12 cal Row
9 Burpee Box Jump Overs
Rest
Goal: Keep a sustainable but aggressive pace and recover quickly during the rest minute.
Coaching notes:
Wall Balls: aim for big, unbroken sets if possible.
Row: strong pulls, but don’t completely blow up the legs.
Burpee Box Jump Overs: steady rhythm, no unnecessary pauses.
Use the rest minute to bring the breathing back under control.
