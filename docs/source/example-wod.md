# Full Body Partner Workout – 50 Minutes

**Total:** 5 min warm-up + 40 min WOD + 5 min cool-down = 50 minutes (excluding the pre-start countdown and any Ready waits between sections).

## Warm-up (5 min)

Both partners together, 1 min per movement, no transitions.

- 1 min Jumping Jacks
- 1 min Air Squats
- 1 min Arm Circles & Shoulder Mobility
- 1 min Alternating Lunges
- 1 min Inchworms

## WOD (40 min)

**Format:** 4 blocks × 10 min. Each block is 2 rounds of 4 exercises at 40 sec work / 20 sec transition, followed by 2 min rest.  
**Timing per block:** 2 rounds × 4:00 (4 × 40 s work + 4 × 20 s transition, including the transition after the last exercise) + 2:00 rest = 10:00.  
**Equipment:** Bodyweight + Dumbbells  
**Setup:** Partner A stays left, Partner B stays right. Round 1: A performs the Bodyweight column, B the Dumbbells column. Round 2: columns swap during the last transition of round 1, so each partner does both columns once per block. Partners keep their positions; only the task columns swap.  
**Block rest:** Every block, including Block 4, ends with its 2 min rest.

### Block 1 – Legs & Strength (10 min)

| Bodyweight | Dumbbells |
|---|---|
| Air Squats | Dumbbell Thrusters |
| Jump Squats | Goblet Squats |
| Mountain Climbers | Romanian Deadlifts |
| Burpees | Alternating Lunges |

### Block 2 – Upper Body & Arms (10 min)

| Bodyweight | Dumbbells |
|---|---|
| Push-ups | Bent-over Rows |
| Plank Shoulder Taps | Shoulder Press |
| Triceps Dips | Biceps Curls |
| Burpees | Overhead Triceps Extensions |

### Block 3 – Abs & Core (10 min)

| Bodyweight | Dumbbells |
|---|---|
| Sit-ups | Russian Twists |
| Bicycle Crunches | Dumbbell Deadlifts |
| Plank | Farmer March |
| Mountain Climbers | Dumbbell Woodchoppers |

### Block 4 – HYROX Finisher (10 min)

| Bodyweight | Dumbbells |
|---|---|
| High Knees | Dumbbell Hang Cleans |
| Skater Jumps | Dumbbell Thrusters |
| Burpees | Alternating Lunges |
| Jump Squats | Biceps Curls |

---

## Cool-down (5 min)

Both partners together, 1 min per movement, no transitions.

- 1 min Quad Stretch
- 1 min Hamstring Stretch
- 1 min Hip Flexor Stretch
- 1 min Chest & Shoulder Stretch
- 1 min Deep Breathing
