
🏃‍♂️ Conditioning & Metcons (The Classics)

• Karen: 150 Wall-Ball Shots for time (20 lb ball to 10 ft target for men / 14 lb ball to 9 ft target for women). A pure test of muscular endurance, leg stamina, and mental fortitude. Elite athletes aim to finish this unbroken in under 5 minutes.
• Helen: 3 Rounds for time of a 400m Run, 21 Kettlebell Swings (24kg / 16kg), and 12 Pull-ups. Widely regarded as a perfectly balanced CrossFit workout that tests the aerobic and glycolytic pathways without overloading the athlete with massive weights.
• Fran: 21-15-9 repetitions for time of Thrusters (95 lbs / 65 lbs) and Pull-ups. The most famous—and feared—CrossFit sprint. It tests high-intensity cardiovascular threshold and raw power output.
• Cindy: A 20-minute AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible) of 5 Pull-ups, 10 Push-ups, and 15 Air Squats. A pure bodyweight gymnastics test measuring baseline endurance and continuous capacity.
• Nancy: 5 Rounds for time of a 400m Run and 15 Overhead Squats (95 lbs / 65 lbs). Tests core stability and leg strength under cardiovascular fatigue.

🏋️‍♂️ Strength & Power Benchmarks

• Grace: 30 Clean and Jerks for time (135 lbs for men / 95 lbs for women). A rapid power endurance test that challenges Olympic lifting technique under dynamic fatigue.
• Isabel: 30 Snatches for time (135 lbs / 95 lbs). Similar to Grace, but utilizing the high-skill snatch movement to test pure speed-strength.
• Diane: 21-15-9 repetitions for time of Deadlifts (225 lbs / 155 lbs) and Handstand Push-ups. A classic pairing of heavy lower-body pulling and upper-body vertical pushing gymnastics.
